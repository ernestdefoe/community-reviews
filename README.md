# Community Reviews

A catalog of products your members review, for Flarum 2. Feature for feature with the MyBB Community Reviews plugin:

- Categories, each with its own criteria. Every review gives each criterion 1–5 stars and a comment.
- "General thoughts", price paid, a link to buy, and the merchant it was bought from (a Merchants group).
- Photos (stored on your forum, or on imgur), with a thumbnail you pick.
- Comments on each product, which can reply to a review.
- View counts, sorting by name, rating, reviews, views or date, and search.
- Merge duplicate products, and report products, reviews and comments to moderators.
- A Reviews tab on profiles: what a member wrote, and for merchants, the reviews that tag them with their average.

Extras, all off by default: helpful votes, verified owners, pros and cons, compare, rich results for search engines,
approval queues, one review per member.

```sh
composer require ernestdefoe/community-reviews
```
